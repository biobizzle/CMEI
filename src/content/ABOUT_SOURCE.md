# CMEI About and public-page content source

Source: **CMEI Business Plan & Partnership Proposal**, planning edition July 2026, prepared by Austin Emmanuel-Omolere (25-page PDF; section 02 pp. 4–7, programme section pp. 8–10, website section pp. 17–22).

## Public facts supported by the document
- Organisation: Creative Media Empowerment Initiative (CMEI), youth-focused creative-media opportunity platform.
- Audience: aspiring photographers, videographers, content creators and digital storytellers aged 16–35.
- Problem: barriers in training, equipment access, structured practice, mentorship, and paths into paid work.
- Practice-first pathway: practical instruction, assessed portfolio-ready projects, mentoring and enterprise preparation.
- Vision, mission and values are kept verbatim or closely paraphrased in `src/content/about.ts`.
- The initial programme is a **proposed** five-day cohort for **20–30** learners. Do not claim it has happened or that applications have opened.
- The official film on the website is a **Creddy Production associated with CMEI through its convener**, not a confirmed CMEI-produced film.

## Claims deliberately withheld until verified
- Actual launched/completed cohorts, alumni, work placements, impact statistics, paid work outcomes, dates, venue, legal/registration status, partner commitments, sponsorship values and testimonials.
- The strategy document contains budget and sponsorship guides; those are **proposals**, not public approved rates.
- The plan calls for team and governance information; `src/content/site.ts` carries the four confirmed public-facing team roles. Do not publish unverified advisers or board structure.

## Page role contracts
- `/about`: why CMEI exists, mission, vision, values, team and proposed pathway. Primary CTA leads to `/approach`.
- `/partner`: precise ways to contribute and direct partnership enquiry to the official contact email. No circular CTA or invented form.
- `/work`: attributed productions and approved creator work. Do not duplicate the Films catalogue.
- `/films`: listing of film entries. Do not render a link to the current listing labelled “View films”.
- Homepage retains approved cinematic hero and full footer; internal routes render a compact utility footer.
- `/films/the-dark-didnt-win`: **preserve approved film page visual composition**, teaser handling and release state.
